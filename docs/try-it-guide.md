# Try Attesto — 5 minutes, no local setup

1. **Get a Solana wallet on devnet.** Install [Phantom](https://phantom.com)
   or [Solflare](https://solflare.com), then switch its network to
   **Devnet** (Settings → Developer Settings → Change Network).
2. **Get devnet SOL** (for transaction fees, ~0.01 SOL is plenty):
   [faucet.solana.com](https://faucet.solana.com), paste your wallet
   address.
3. **Get a little devnet USDC.** This is the one non-obvious step — ping
   us (Discord/Telegram/whatever we're using) with your devnet wallet
   address and we'll send you enough for a few skill-checks (~$0.03).
   *(Circle's own faucet may also support Solana Devnet USDC directly —
   worth trying at [faucet.circle.com](https://faucet.circle.com) first;
   we haven't independently confirmed Solana devnet is listed there today,
   so if it's not, just ask us.)*
4. **Go to [attesto.xyz](https://attesto.xyz), click "Try it."** Paste any
   Solana address (there's an example button if you don't have one handy),
   click "Get price."
5. **Check the box, connect your wallet, click "Pay ... USDC & get
   attestation."** One signature. You'll get a score and two links to a
   Solana explorer — the fulfillment receipt and the payment transaction —
   so you can verify everything yourself, independent of anything we tell
   you.

That's it. No account, no email, nothing installed beyond a wallet
extension.
